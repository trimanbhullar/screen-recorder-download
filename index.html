Set it up

Recording in a few minutes

  1. Add your access key above and download Screen-Recorder.exe. It's a single file with nothing to install, so you can keep it anywhere.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Paste your access key when it asks. The first time, it downloads its video engine (about 110 MB), once.
  4. Pick Demo, Share or Long call, choose a screen or a window, and press Ctrl+Shift+R. The window hides to the tray and a small timer sits above the clock. Press Ctrl+Shift+R again to stop and get your MP4.
Why the warning? The app isn't registered with Microsoft yet, so Windows shows a warning for new apps the first time. You only need to approve it once.

What it does

Simple from the first click

One click to record

Pick a screen or one window and press Record, or Ctrl+Shift+R from anywhere. The recorder hides to the tray, and right-click menus and dropdowns are captured too.

Easy settings

Everything fits on one screen: a big preview of what you’ll record and two tiles for your microphone and your computer’s sound. Nothing to set up before your first recording.

Presets that do the tuning

Demo for the sharpest text, Share for a good balance of quality and size, Long call for the smallest files. Each one saves an MP4 that plays in Slack, Teams, Drive and browsers.

No limits

Record for as long as you like, as often as you like. No time limits, no watermarks on your videos, and every file is yours to keep.

Stays out of the shot

When recording starts, the window hides to the tray and a small timer with the size in MB sits above the clock. Neither appears in recordings or screen shares.

Your voice and the call

Your microphone and everything you hear, like the people on a call, apps and the browser, mixed together with echo cancellation. Pause, resume or discard at any time.
